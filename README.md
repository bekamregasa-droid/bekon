# Bekon - Career Discovery App

Bekon helps you discover careers that align with your natural strengths and personality through AI-powered assessments and personalized recommendations.

## Features

-  **Cognitive Assessment Games** - 6 different games measuring various abilities
-  **Personalized Results** - Detailed analysis of your strengths
-  **Career Matching** - Find careers that match your profile
-  **Progress Tracking** - Monitor your development over time
-  **User Profile** - Save and manage your preferences
-  **Comprehensive Settings** - Customize your experience

## Screens

1. **Splash Screen** - App launch with animations
2. **Welcome Screen** - Introduction to the app
3. **Authentication** - Login, Sign Up, Forgot Password
4. **Assessment Intro** - Explanation of the assessment process
5. **Aptitude Games** - 6 interactive cognitive games
6. **Results Screen** - Detailed assessment results
7. **Dashboard** - Main app hub with Home, Explore, History, Profile
8. **Career Details** - In-depth career information
9. **Profile** - User profile with stats and achievements
10. **Statistics** - Visual data tracking
11. **Settings Suite** - App configuration screens
